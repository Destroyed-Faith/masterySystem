# Destroyed Faith Media and Community Use Policy

We want people to play, stream, discuss, review, and share Destroyed Faith.

This policy explains what players, streamers, reviewers, content creators, professional game masters, and convention organizers may do with Destroyed Faith and the Mastery System without asking for separate permission.

The official Foundry system remains proprietary. See [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md).

## Expressly permitted

You may:

- livestream Destroyed Faith;
- publish recorded Actual Plays;
- publish YouTube and other video-platform content;
- stream on Twitch and comparable platforms;
- produce podcasts;
- publish reviews;
- explain rules;
- create character-creation tutorials;
- demonstrate Foundry use of the system;
- share screenshots;
- post about the game on social media;
- demonstrate the game at conventions and similar events;
- run public game sessions;
- run free and paid game-mastering services;
- use ordinary platform monetization for the above.

Ordinary monetization includes advertising revenue, platform revenue, sponsorships, subscriptions, donations, tips, and crowdfunding connected to an individual show or channel, provided the Destroyed Faith system or its protected assets are not themselves being sold.

## In-context display

In permitted coverage, you may normally display:

- the Foundry interface;
- character sheets;
- maps during play;
- tokens during play;
- rulebook pages shown briefly for explanation or review;
- artwork visible during gameplay or in official project interfaces;
- short quotations needed for criticism, review, tutorial, or explanation.

## Names

You may use the names **Destroyed Faith** and **Mastery System** solely to identify the game being played or discussed.

Do not imply that your content, product, or service is official, partnered, sponsored, endorsed, or approved by the Destroyed Faith project unless you have prior written authorization.

## Conditions

Where reasonably practical, please:

- identify the game as Destroyed Faith;
- avoid implying official partnership, sponsorship, endorsement, or approval;
- clearly label unofficial fan material as unofficial;
- comply with platform rules and applicable law;
- respect the privacy and rights of participating players;
- use a legally licensed copy of Foundry VTT where Foundry is shown or used.

Suggested attribution (simple and non-burdensome):

> Destroyed Faith and the Mastery System are created by Daniel Rodrigo Navarro Melendo. This content is unofficial and is not endorsed by the Destroyed Faith project.

Where practical, link to the official website or repository. A missing link does not automatically invalidate otherwise good-faith media coverage.

## What is not permitted

This media permission does **not** allow you to:

- upload or distribute the complete rulebook;
- read or reproduce the entire rulebook as an audiobook or substitute publication;
- distribute the Foundry system ZIP;
- publish a modified Foundry system;
- extract and redistribute full-resolution artwork, maps, tokens, icons, logos, or other assets;
- create asset packs from repository content;
- sell the game’s artwork on merchandise;
- sell copies or derivatives of the system;
- present unofficial products as official;
- use the project’s identity to mislead consumers;
- publish content that violates third-party rights.

## Thumbnails and promotional use

You may use:

- screenshots from actual gameplay;
- screenshots from the official website or Foundry system;
- cropped gameplay imagery;
- official images specifically designated as press or community assets.

This policy does **not** automatically grant standalone reuse rights for every full-resolution commissioned artwork.

If the project later provides files in a designated `press-kit/` or `community-assets/` directory, those files may be used under the terms stated in that directory.

## Monetization

Ordinary monetization of permitted media is allowed and does **not** require separate permission.

This includes monetized reviews, Actual Plays, tutorials, streams, podcasts, sponsored videos, and paid GM sessions.

You may earn money from your own performance, commentary, production, or game-mastering service.

You may **not** sell or sublicense the Destroyed Faith system, source code, rulebook, artwork, maps, tokens, or other protected project materials themselves.

## Relationship to the software license

This policy supplements [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md). It does not make the project open source and does not authorize public modified distributions of the official core.
