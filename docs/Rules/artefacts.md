## Artifacts

> **Hunter's Scourge (Core-only):** The Relic Hunter Echo Artifact is defined in the current Core, not separately reprinted in this standalone catalogue. Hunter's Scourge grants **Vitality: Damage Maximization Stone Power Support** at Artifact Levels 2 / 5 / 8 (Ranks 2 / 3 / 4). The normal **Limited Scourge Strike may be maximized** using one prepared Damage Maximization use. Refer to the current Core for the definitive mechanic.

> **CURRENT ARTIFACT CATALOGUE:** These are the approved standalone Artifact profiles for v0.9.9.5. The Core supplies the universal Artifact, Stone, Transformation, and Awakening rules; the current four Power catalogues supply the named Powers. Echo Artifacts printed in Core use their Core tables. All Artifact Level 10 effects use Artifact Awakening.

> **Catalogue scope:** Artifact Levels 1–10 and their listed base profiles are the current profiles. Historical migration notes, retired combined Powers and old Ultimate drafts are archived separately.

> **Named slot exception:** Hunter's Scourge in the current Core is the explicitly approved Hand-slot exception for **Vitality: Damage Maximization Stone Power Support**. It does not grant Vitality Stones or bypass the normal need to unlock the Ability. This does not change the permitted Attributes of ordinary Hand Artifact Stone Functions.

Artifacts are bound objects, inherited manifestations, living relics, forged weapons, awakened armor, cursed tokens, supernatural body features, or similar relics that occupy real Equipment Slots.

Artifacts are not free-form construction tools. They have an Artifact Capacity cost, one or more Equipment Slots, a Base Profile, a fixed Level Progression, and a limited list of Artifact Functions.

Players do not freely buy any effect they want through an Artifact. They use the functions written into that Artifact.

Echo Artifacts follow the same rules, but they are part of a character's origin and cannot normally be removed.

---

#### Core Rules

By default, a character can be bound to up to **4 Artifacts** at the same time.

To bind an Artifact, both requirements must be met:

1. The character must have free Artifact Capacity.
2. The required Equipment Slot or Slots must be free.

Echo Artifacts count against Artifact Capacity.

Humans normally begin with no fixed Echo Artifacts and therefore keep their full Artifact Capacity open.

---

#### Equipment Slots

| Slot | Use |
|---|---|
| **Main Hand** | Main weapon, one-handed weapon, shield, hand focus, natural weapon |
| **Off Hand** | Shield, second weapon, offhand weapon, secondary hand item |
| **Body** | Armor, robes, natural armor, scales, plating, armored skin, body transformation |
| **Head** | Helmet, crown, mask, horns, eyes, senses, breath source, reaction focus |
| **Feet** | Boots, hooves, talons, movement organs, terrain movement |
| **Amulet** | Necklace, soul chain, oath mark, curse mark, spiritual binding |
| **Ring** | One magical ring, seal, minor binding, small ward, focused support |

The following Slots do not exist:

- Hands
- Core
- Focus
- Cape
- Belt
- Legs
- Trinket

Hands are represented by **Main Hand** and **Off Hand**.

A Focus is a function, not a Slot. If an Artifact functions as a focus, it must still occupy a real Slot.

### Binding and Awakening an Artifact

A newly acquired Artifact is initially **dormant**.

To use it, the character must bind the Artifact through an **Attunement Ritual**. The ritual permanently establishes the bond between character and Artifact, but does **not** permanently bind, Seal, Burn, or otherwise reserve a Stone.

Once the Attunement Ritual is completed, the Artifact awakens at **Artifact Level 1**.

**Artifact Level 1 is free.** No XP is required to awaken an Artifact for the first time.

The Artifact immediately counts against the character's **Artifact Capacity** and occupies its required **Equipment Slot or Slots**.

---

### Artifact Progression

Artifacts have **10 Artifact Levels**. Level 10 grants the universal **Artifact Awakening** rule in the current Core, using the existing Power and Stone mechanics. The historical bespoke Ultimate drafts are not used.

As an Artifact grows, it may improve its Base Profile, unlock new Powers, or strengthen existing functions. At Level 10, use the universal Artifact Awakening rule in the current Core.

Players do not freely purchase arbitrary effects for an Artifact. Each Artifact has a fixed mechanical structure that represents its identity, history, and supernatural nature.

#### Artifact Level Cap by Mastery Rank

| **Mastery Rank** | **Maximum Artifact Level** |
|:--:|:--:|
| **MR 1** | **Level 1** |
| **MR 2** | **Level 2** |
| **MR 3** | **Level 4** |
| **MR 4** | **Level 6** |
| **MR 5** | **Level 8** |
| **MR 6+** | **Level 10** |

A character may not **increase** an Artifact beyond the maximum Artifact Level allowed by their current Mastery Rank, even if they have enough XP. If Mastery Rank is later reduced, an already awakened Artifact does not automatically lose Levels; it simply cannot be advanced further until its Level is legal again.

#### Artifact Advancement Costs

| **Artifact Advancement** | **XP Cost** |
|:--|:--:|
| Bind and awaken a new Artifact at Level 1 | **Free** |
| Increase an Artifact to Level 2 or 3 | **8 XP per Level** |
| Increase an Artifact to Level 4, 5, or 6 | **16 XP per Level** |
| Increase an Artifact to Level 7, 8, or 9 | **32 XP per Level** |
| Increase an Artifact to Level 10 | **64 XP** |

An Artifact may be increased by **multiple Levels during the same Upgrade Step**, provided the character pays the XP cost of each Level gained and does not exceed the Artifact Level Cap for their current Mastery Rank. No one-Level-per-Upgrade-Step restriction applies.

The XP cost is determined by the **new Artifact Level reached**, not by the Artifact's current Level.

#### Slot Blocking

If an Artifact occupies an Equipment Slot, that Slot is fully blocked.

A blocked Slot cannot hold another Artifact or mundane equipment.

If an Artifact occupies multiple Equipment Slots, all of those Slots are blocked.

Examples:

- A one-handed Artifact Weapon occupies Main Hand or Off Hand.
- A two-handed Artifact Weapon occupies Main Hand and Off Hand.
- Dragon Claws occupy Main Hand and Off Hand.
- Dragon Scales occupy Body.
- A magical ring occupies Ring. A character can bind only one magical Ring.

---

#### Two-Handed Artifacts

A two-handed Artifact counts as **one Artifact** for Artifact Capacity.

It occupies both **Main Hand** and **Off Hand**.

Because it permanently occupies both hand Slots, a two-handed Artifact may contain up to **three Base Values** and may therefore use **Base Value C**.

A one-handed or versatile Artifact does not gain access to Base Value C merely because it is temporarily held or used with both hands.

A character with a two-handed Artifact cannot use another weapon, shield, hand focus, claw Artifact, or hand-based magical item at the same time.

| Artifact | Artifact Capacity | Occupied Slots |
|---|---:|---|
| One-Handed Sword | 1 | Main Hand |
| Shield | 1 | Off Hand |
| Greatsword | 1 | Main Hand + Off Hand |
| Dragon Claws | 1 | Main Hand + Off Hand |
| Twin Shield Artifact | 1 | Main Hand + Off Hand |

Two shields are not part of the standard Artifact rules yet.

The GM may create a special Twin Shield Artifact if it fits the character concept.

---

#### Echo Artifacts

Echo Artifacts are predefined Artifacts granted by a character's Echo.

Players do not build Echo Artifacts freely.

Each Echo lists which Echo Artifacts are required or available.

Echo Artifacts count against Artifact Capacity, occupy real Equipment Slots, and cannot normally be removed, unequipped, sold, stolen, replaced, or unbound.

Example: If your Echo grants Dragon Scales, those Scales occupy your Body Slot. You cannot decide to stop having Dragon Scales in order to free the Body Slot.

### Base Profiles and Artifact Functions

Every Artifact has a **Base Profile**.

The Base Profile defines what the Artifact is before supernatural progression is added.

The Base Profile is not a Power. It does not count as an Active, Active Buff, Movement Power, Passive, Reaction, or Stone Function.

Base values belong in the Base table:

- Weapon Damage
- Armor Base
- Shield Value
- Head Armor
- Feet movement qualities
- similar slot values

Sense Options are listed separately. They are never Base Values.

The Level Progression table is for unlocked Artifact Functions.

---

## Base Item Values

Each Artifact has a Base Item.

The Base Item may contain up to three Base Values.

Base Values are not Power Lines.

They do not count as Actives, Active Buffs, Movement Powers, Passives, Reactions, or Stone Functions.

They define what the Artifact physically or inherently is.

| Base Value | Unlock | Improvement | Final Improvement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Base Value A** | Level 1 | Level 4 | Level 7 |
| **Base Value B** | Level 4 | Level 7 | — |
| **Base Value C** | Level 7 | — | — |

Base Value A is the Artifact's primary identity.

Base Value B is a secondary feature.

Base Value C is a late base feature.

Artifact Level 10 grants Artifact Awakening; it does not create a new Ultimate Base Value.

### Base Value Limits by Slot

Each Artifact has a Base Item.

A Base Item may contain one or more Base Values, but the maximum number of Base Values is determined by the Artifact's occupied Slot.

Base Values are not Power Lines.

They do not count as Actives, Active Buffs, Movement Powers, Passives, Reactions, or Stone Functions.

They define what the Artifact physically or inherently is.

| Slot | Maximum Base Values |
|---|---:|
| **Main Hand / Off Hand** | 2 |
| **Main Hand + Off Hand** | 3 |
| **Body** | 1 |
| **Head** | 2 |
| **Feet** | 2 |
| **Ring** | 3 |
| **Amulet** | 3 |

---
**The combined maximum number of Base Values printed on an equipped Ring and Amulet is 4.**

The Slot determines the Base Value limit.

The Artifact's story, appearance, origin, rarity, Echo status, or binding type does not change this limit.

A one-handed Weapon, Shield, Claw, Gauntlet, Hand Focus, or similar Artifact that occupies only one hand Slot may have up to **two Base Values**.

A two-handed Artifact that permanently occupies both **Main Hand** and **Off Hand** may have up to **three Base Values** and may use Base Value C.

A Body Artifact may have only one Base Value, regardless of whether it is armor, scales, robes, skin, plating, clothing, or another body-based form. A Body Armor Base Value may contain its linked **Armor and Evade values** as defined by the Body Armor progression. That linked defensive profile still counts as one Base Value.

A Head Artifact may have up to two Base Values.

A Feet Artifact may have up to two Base Values.

A Ring or Amulet may individually have up to **three Base Values**.

An equipped Ring and equipped Amulet share a combined maximum of **four printed Base Values**. Count every Base Value assigned to either Artifact, including Base Values that have not yet unlocked through Artifact Levels.

The legal distributions are:

- Ring 3 + Amulet 1
- Ring 2 + Amulet 2
- Ring 1 + Amulet 3

Combinations such as 3 + 2, 3 + 3, or 4 + 0 are not legal. If only one of the two Slots is occupied, that Artifact is still limited to three Base Values.

Echo Artifacts do not receive additional Base Values.
Echo-bound means the Artifact is part of the character's origin and cannot normally be removed, replaced, sold, stolen, or unbound. It does not increase the Artifact's Base Value limit.
If an Artifact needs additional functionality beyond its allowed Base Values, that functionality must be written into the Level Progression table.

### Allowed Base Values

| Base Value Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | Weapon Damage, Bite Damage, Claw Damage, Tail Damage |
| **Armor** | Body Armor defensive profile, Head Armor, Feet Minor Armor, Shield Value |
| **Evade** | linked Body Armor Evade progression, minor Head or Feet Evade |
| **Movement** | walking, climbing, swimming, tunneling, flying; **Immovable on Feet Base Value B only** (declare during Stone allocation; lasts 1 Round) |
| **Thrown Range** | thrown weapon range |
| **Weapon Special** | Penetration, Challenge, Corrode, Disoriented, Expose, Hex, Lacerate, Mark, Ruin, Slow, Soulburn, Sundered, Weaken, Push, Brutal Impact, Finesse |
| **Minor Slot Feature** | Returning, Spell Focus, Shadow Stealth, Breath Interaction |

A Base Value must fit the Artifact's Slot.

A Base Value is never a full Active, Active Buff, Reaction, Stone Function, or Movement Power.

If it creates an action, trigger, resource use, or maintained state, it belongs in Level Progression, not in the Base Item.

---

### Base Value Limit

An Artifact may not have more than three Base Values.

If a Base Value is unusually strong, such as Returning + Thrown together or a scaling Special package, it may count as two Base Values.

A Base Value cannot secretly contain a Power Line.

### Artifact Level Structure

| Artifact Level | Stage |
|---:|---|
| 1-3 | Basic |
| 4-6 | Improved |
| 7-9 | Greater |
| 10 | Ultimate |

When an Artifact grants a normal Power through its Level Progression, use the following default Power Levels:

| Artifact Stage | Power Level |
|---|---:|
| Basic | Power Level 4 |
| Improved | Power Level 10 |
| Greater | Power Level 16 |

Artifact Level 10 grants the universal Artifact Awakening benefits defined in the current Core.

---

### Technical References and Power Identity

The **Artifact Name** is the character-facing name of an Artifact Function. The **Technical Reference** is its mechanical identity for rules interactions and Foundry VTT.

The Artifact Name remains visible. The Technical Reference is recorded separately and may be shown in a tooltip or expanded rules view.

**PL** means effective Power Level. Power Level, Artifact stage, source, and source restrictions are not part of the base Power identity.

- If a Technical Reference names a catalogue Power, use that Power's complete rules at the listed PL.
- An explicit Artifact restriction may limit the catalogue Power to the Artifact, its weapon, its Range, a fixed target, a fixed Trigger, or another written delivery profile.
- **New/revised Artifact Functions must use an existing exact catalogue Power or Stone Ability.** Do not invent Artifact-specific Damage or Special potency. An explicitly printed Artifact-specific exception remains limited to that Artifact. New functions must match a current catalogue or Stone Ability.
- Successive stages of one Artifact Function use the same base Technical Reference. Stage numerals such as I, II, and III remain part of the Artifact Name only. Changing PL, capacity, distance, uses, or another parenthetical source-profile value does not create a new base identity.
- **Artifact Active**, **Artifact Active Buff**, and **Artifact Reaction** are Artifact-exclusive Powers. They follow the normal Power Use Limit under their exact base Technical Reference.
- **Artifact Function**, **Stone Power Support**, **Stone Battery**, **Sense Option**, **Base Completion**, and **Ultimate** are not catalogue Powers unless their Technical Reference explicitly names one.
- The retired bespoke Ultimate drafts do not grant additional effects. Artifact Level 10 follows the current Core's universal Artifact Awakening rule.

If an Artifact summary conflicts with a referenced catalogue Power, the catalogue Power controls except for an explicit Artifact override written into that source profile.

#### Duplicate Power Sources

A Power's identity is its base **Technical Reference**, not its Artifact Name, character-facing name, source, or current PL.

If several sources grant the same base Technical Reference, they grant one Power with several source profiles.

- Duplicate sources do not stack.
- Duplicate sources do not grant another use of the Power.
- Duplicate sources do not bypass the once-per-round Power Use Limit or any other use restriction.
- All source profiles share the same spent state until that state normally resets.
- Use the highest currently available and legal Power Level among those source profiles.
- If the highest profile cannot legally be used because its Artifact, weapon, target, Range, Trigger, or other source restriction is unavailable, use the highest remaining legal profile.
- A later Artifact row with the same base Technical Reference is an upgrade of the same Artifact source. It replaces the earlier Artifact profile and never creates another copy.

A character cannot intentionally purchase or select a second copy of the same base Technical Reference to gain another use.

---

#### Artifact Overlap and Retraining

If an Artifact first grants a Technical Power that the character has already purchased with their own advancement resources, the character may choose one of the following options:

1. Keep the purchased version as a fallback source.
2. Retrain the purchased Power and its paid upgrades at no additional cost during the next legal retraining or downtime opportunity.

The replacement choices must follow all normal requirements and may not exceed the total advancement value actually paid for the replaced Power and its upgrades.

This retraining grants no bonus XP, Power Points, or other advancement resources. A Power received for free has no refundable or retrainable advancement value.

If the purchased version is retained and the Artifact becomes unavailable, the character falls back to the purchased profile at its own PL. If the purchased version was retrained, the character loses access to the Power while the Artifact is unavailable.

Foundry VTT must never delete, refund, or retrain a purchased Power automatically. It should identify the overlap, combine the source profiles for use tracking, and show the retraining choice to the player and GM.

### Reaction Rules for Artifacts

If an Artifact grants a Reaction, it follows all normal Reaction rules.

A Reaction answers one immediate trigger.

A Reaction normally applies only to the triggering attack, hit, damage instance, defensive event, or declared Trigger.

If the referenced Reaction explicitly gives an effect a different duration, that specific duration controls. For example, remaining Temporary HP from **Reaction: Temporary HP** lasts until the end of the user's next Turn, and Initiative gained through **Reaction: Initiative** follows its own permanent Initiative rules.

A Reaction is not an Active Buff or a Support Power. It creates no maintained state **unless its exact Technical Reference explicitly says otherwise**.

---

#### Duplicate Reaction Sources

Reactions follow the universal **Duplicate Power Sources** rule above.

The chosen Trigger is stored separately and does not create a second copy of the same Reaction Effect. If multiple sources grant the same base Reaction Effect with different Triggers, they share one spent state and use the highest currently legal source profile.

A character still normally has only one Reaction per Round.

## Returning Weapon Baseline

If an Artifact Weapon is written as **Returning**, it returns to the wielder after a thrown attack or thrown Active granted by that Artifact is resolved.

Returning does not grant an additional attack.

Returning does not change the action cost of attacks or Actives.

Returning does not allow the wielder to bypass normal range, target, or line of sight rules.

Returning only applies to attacks or Actives performed with that Artifact.

---

## Weapon Special Baseline

Weaken and Soulburn use the Start PP 8 weapon progression. They reduce dice pools, not Attributes, Keep, or derived values.

An Artifact Weapon may gain one numeric Weapon Special through its Base or custom Weapon table.

Numeric Weapon Specials use the listed baseline groups. **Blight is Timed**, not Diminishing; its printed X counts affected-creature Turns. Challenge and the direct pool-reduction Specials have compressed costs in the current AGENT and therefore require a separate budget review before old Artifact weapon values are treated as up-to-date.

| Special Group | Eligible Specials | Level 1 | Level 4 | Level 7 | Level 10 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Start PP 3** | Blight | 2 | 4 | 7 | 9 |
| **Start PP 4** | Lacerate, Mark, Ruin, Slow | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| **Start PP 6** | Challenge, Corrode, Hex, Sundered | 1 | 3 | 4 | 6 |
| **Start PP 8** | Disoriented, Expose, Soulburn, Weaken | 1 | 2 | 4 | 5 |

Use these separate baselines for Instant Specials:

| Special | Level 1 | Level 4 | Level 7 | Level 10 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Penetration | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Push / Pull | 2 m | 4 m | 6 m | 8 m |
| Precision | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Brutal Impact | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 |

Qualitative traits such as Finesse, Light, Reach, Thrown, Returning, and Spell Focus do not scale by number.

An Artifact Weapon normally carries only one full-value scaling Special. If it carries several, reduce their values or stagger their progression.

## Hand Artifacts

Hand Artifacts represent anything used through the hands.

This includes weapons, shields, natural weapons, claws, hand foci, and similar hand-based Artifacts.

Hand Artifacts use only three Base Profiles:

1. **One-Handed Weapon**
2. **Two-Handed Weapon**
3. **Shield**

Parry tools, paired weapons, claws, gauntlets, weapon foci, and similar concepts must be written as one of these three profiles unless the GM creates a specific exception.

A shield used offensively is still a Shield.

---

#### Hand Artifact Base Profiles

| Profile | Slots | Artifact Capacity | Base Function |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **One-Handed Weapon** | Main Hand or Off Hand | 1 | Weapon Damage plus up to one additional eligible Base Value |
| **Two-Handed Weapon** | Main Hand + Off Hand | 1 | Weapon Damage plus up to two additional eligible Base Values |
| **Shield** | Main Hand or Off Hand | 1 | Shield Armor plus up to one additional eligible Base Value |

---

#### Weapon Actives

Main Hand and Off Hand Artifacts may grant Actives.

These Actives must be tied to the Artifact.

A Hand Artifact Active may include:

- Weapon Damage
- Healing
- Cleanse
- Dispel
- Specials
- offensive spell-like effects
- shield strikes
- guard strikes
- other direct hand-based effects

A Hand Artifact Active may not grant:

- defensive Active Buffs
- Armor as a main defensive package
- Damage Reduction
- Phasing
- Movement
- general Passive support
- unrelated defensive systems

When a character has multiple Hand Artifacts, each Active must be performed with one specific Artifact.

Effects from one Artifact do not apply to an Active performed with another Artifact unless a rule explicitly says so.

## Body Armor Baseline

Magical Body Armor is built from a normal Armor chassis.

Choose **Light Armor**, **Medium Armor**, or **Heavy Armor** when the Artifact is created. A Robe is handled separately under **Robe Artifacts**.

The chosen Armor type determines the Artifact's starting Armor, its normal Evade modifier, and any Initiative or Skill penalties.

| Body Base | Starting Armor | Base Evade Modifier | Initiative | Skill Penalty |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Robe / No Armor | 0 | +0 | - | - |
| Light Armor | 4 | +0 | - | - |
| Medium Armor | 8 | -2 | -4 | -1d8 Physical Skills |
| Heavy Armor | 12 | -4 | -8 | -2d8 Physical Skills |

The armor being natural, grown, forged, summoned, worn, made of shadow, or represented by another supernatural form does not remove its Armor-type drawbacks unless the Artifact explicitly says so.

---

#### Building Body Armor Artifacts

Body Armor Artifacts do **not** add a separate universal Artifact Armor Bonus to mundane Armor.

Instead, every Body Armor Artifact uses the complete Level progression of its chosen Armor type below.

- **Light Armor** uses linked Armor and Evade progression. Armor begins at **4** and reaches **8**. Evade begins at **+2** and increases by **+1 every Artifact Level**, reaching **+11** at Level 10.
- **Medium Armor** uses linked Armor and Evade progression. Armor begins at **8** and reaches **12**. Its Artifact Evade Bonus begins at **+1** and increases by **+1 every two Artifact Levels**, reaching **+5**. The normal Medium Armor **-2 Evade** modifier still applies.
- **Heavy Armor** is primarily Armor. Armor begins at **12** and reaches **16**. It keeps its normal **-4 Evade** modifier, but from Artifact Level 6 onward it gains a fixed **+2 Artifact Evade Bonus**, reducing the final Armor-based modifier to **-2 Evade**.

For all three Armor types, Armor increases by **+1 every two Artifact Levels** after Level 2.

The Armor and Evade entries printed in these tables are one linked **Body Armor Base Value**. They do not consume separate Base Value slots.

The listed Armor is the **total Armor provided by the Body Artifact at that Artifact Level**. Do not add the mundane Starting Armor a second time.

For Medium and Heavy Armor, calculate the final Evade modifier by combining the Armor type's normal Base Evade Modifier with the Artifact Evade Bonus shown in its progression table.

Initiative and Physical Skill penalties remain unchanged at all Artifact Levels unless a specific Artifact explicitly overrides them.

Armor and Evade values from the Body Armor progression cannot be traded, redistributed, or converted into each other.

### Artifact Sense Options

Artifacts may grant **Sense Options**.

A Sense Option is a small perception feature that sits beside the Artifact's real mechanics.

A Sense Option:

- is not a Base Value,
- does not occupy Base Value A, B, or C,
- is not a Power Line,
- is not a priced Artifact Function or Level Progression entry,
- is not a Passive,
- costs no PP,
- and does not scale with Artifact Level.

Armor, Damage, Evade, Movement, Weapon Specials, and other actual Artifact values are built normally. A Sense Option is written separately beneath the Base Item.

An Artifact normally grants no more than one Sense Option unless its entry explicitly says otherwise.

---

### The Character's Sense Slot

Every character has one **Sense Slot**.

By default, that slot contains **Normal Combat Awareness**.

An Artifact, Echo, Species, Power, or story feature may add a Special Combat Sense to the character's available Sense Options. Gaining a Sense Option does not create another Sense Slot.

At the start of a scene, before the first Perception roll, declare which known Sense occupies the Sense Slot. In combat, declare it before Initiative is rolled. If no declaration is made, the slot contains Normal Combat Awareness.

The chosen Sense remains active for the scene or combat. It cannot be switched in response to a failed roll, a newly revealed enemy, or an attack unless a rule explicitly allows the switch.

Selecting a Special Combat Sense does not make the character blind, deaf, or unable to receive ordinary narrative information. For rules that ask whether a Combat Sense can locate or target something through darkness, Invisibility, concealment, smoke, silence, or another Sense-specific obstacle, use the Sense currently occupying the Sense Slot.

### Sense Boundaries

A Sense only perceives what its channel can plausibly detect.

Examples:

- Tremor Sense does not perceive a flying creature that never touches the connected surface.
- Life Sense does not perceive a construct or a lifeless object.
- Mage Sense does not reveal mundane stealth merely because the hidden creature carries no magic.
- Sonar Sense can fail in magical silence or overwhelming noise.
- Predator Sense can fail against scentless constructs, sealed targets, or creatures without readable blood, fear, or weakness.

A Sense does not ignore range, total cover, line of effect, sealed barriers, or a specific effect that blocks that Sense unless its own rule explicitly says otherwise.

---

### Darkvision

Darkvision is a minor sight upgrade, not a full Combat Sense.

It allows Normal Combat Awareness to function through ordinary darkness within its listed range. It does not occupy the Sense Slot and does not reveal invisible creatures, see through smoke or walls, or identify illusions as false.

Darkvision is also written separately from Base Values and Power Lines.

---

### Example: Stonebound Soles

Stonebound Soles may have **Armor** as Base Value A and **Tunneling** as Base Value B.

They additionally grant **Tremor Sense** as a Sense Option.

The Tremor Sense does not replace either Base Value. Before a combat begins, the dwarf decides whether Normal Combat Awareness or Tremor Sense occupies the Sense Slot.

A blind dwarf using Tremor Sense can still fight grounded enemies that create readable vibration. A flying enemy remains a serious problem because Tremor Sense cannot perceive it while it is airborne.

---

### Canonical Sense Rule

Sense Options are deliberately useful but secondary.

They create distinctive concepts and alternative ways to perceive a scene. They do not consume the Artifact's real mechanical values and they do not become a second progression system.

## Minor Movement Base Values

Minor Movement Base Values are additive bonuses to an existing movement mode.

They are not Movement Powers.

They do not replace normal Movement.

They do not grant additional Movement.

They do not create access to a movement mode by themselves unless the movement mode is normal Walking.

They do not prevent movement-triggered Reactions unless they modify an existing Safe Movement Power.

They do not grant damage, Specials, Attack Dice, Critical, Armor, Evade, Temporary HP, defensive Phasing, or Reaction-style avoidance.

If a character cannot currently use the matching movement mode, the bonus has no effect.

#### Special Movement Modes

Special movement modes cost more than normal ground movement.

| Movement Mode | Conversion |
|---|---|
| Walking / Running | Full value |
| Climbing | Full value if surface access matters, otherwise GM may reduce |
| Swimming | Full value in water-heavy campaigns, otherwise GM may reduce |
| Jumping | Use as jump distance or jump height according to the Artifact |
| Tunneling | Half value |
| Flying | Half value |
| Teleport-style Movement | Must be written as a Movement Power, not as Base speed |

Example: If a Feet Artifact grants +4 m normal movement, it may instead grant +2 m Flight if the Artifact is written as a flight Artifact.

Flying, tunneling, and teleport-style movement must be explicitly written into the Artifact.

They are not automatically available to every Feet Artifact.

## Amulet and Ring Baseline

Amulet and Ring Artifacts are support Artifacts.

They may grant:

- Passive Reinforcement
- Ward and Spell Resistance Support
- Actives
- Reactions
- Stone Functions
- soul, oath, curse, ward, seal, spirit, or resource support effects

Amulet and Ring Artifacts may not grant:

- Weapon Damage
- Body Armor
- Movement
- Extra Attacks
- broad weapon-style offensive scaling
- defensive Active Buff Extension

Amulet and Ring use the same general rules.

A character may bind one Amulet and one magical Ring, unless another rule says otherwise.

---

#### Passive Reinforcement

An Amulet or Ring may reinforce one chosen Passive.

| Artifact Level | Passive Reinforcement |
|---:|---:|
| Level 1-3 | Passive counts as +1 level |
| Level 4-6 | Passive counts as +2 levels |
| Level 7-9 | Passive counts as +3 levels |
| Level 10 | Passive counts as +4 levels |

This cannot raise a Passive above its normal maximum.

This does not grant an additional Passive Slot.

This does not turn a Passive into an Active, Active Buff, Movement Power, or Reaction.

## Stone Functions

Artifacts may contain Stone Functions.

Stone Functions are not normal Powers.

They are Artifact resource functions that interact with Stones.

Each Artifact can have at most one Stone Function.

A character cannot benefit from the same Stone Function twice across different bound Artifacts.

If two bound Artifacts would grant the same Stone Function, the character chooses which one is active after a Safe Haven Rest.

The same Stone Function means the same Function applied to the same Stone Power, Attribute, or resource.

Stone Functions must fit the Artifact's Slot.

---

#### Stone Power Support

**The current Core Stone Ability Rank rules apply.** Every Stone Ability has Ranks 1–4. Stone Power Support pre-fills exactly one named **Rank** of one eligible Stone Ability chosen when the Artifact is designed. The supported Rank's *incremental cost* is paid by the Artifact; **all lower Ranks must still be purchased and resolved normally**. The older claim that Crit, Parry, Extra Attack, or other premium Abilities begin at Rank 2 is retired and invalid. **Premium Stone Abilities DO have Rank 1.**

The chosen Stone Ability must fit the Artifact's Slot and theme. A character must have unlocked that Stone Ability normally. Support creates no extra activation or uses, and respects all existing timing, once-per-combat limits, and restrictions of that Stone Ability. The character cannot use unsupported lower Ranks for free. For **Agility Ability: Crit**, the Support only pre-fills its chosen Rank and never independently grants Critical or a Critical Weapon Special.

| Artifact Level | Supported Rank | Lower Ranks still payable | Normal cost of lower Ranks | Premium cost of lower Ranks |
|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Level 1–3 | Rank 2 | Rank 1 | 1 Stone | 2 Stones |
| Level 4–6 | Rank 3 | Ranks 1 and 2 | 3 Stones | 6 Stones |
| Level 7–9 | Rank 4 | Ranks 1, 2 and 3 | 7 Stones | 12 Stones |

The Artifact does not change standard costs. If a specific approved Artifact explicitly imposes an additional rest-based use limit, apply it in addition to the supported Ability's normal timing; do not infer a universal extra MR-times limit from a legacy draft.

---

#### Stone Ability Cost Chains (Core)

| Rank | Normal incremental | Normal cumulative | Premium incremental | Premium cumulative |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rank 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Rank 2 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 6 |
| Rank 3 | 4 | 7 | 6 | 12 |
| Rank 4 | 8 | 15 | 8 | 20 |

Premium Abilities are exactly: Extra Attack, Parry, Crit, Damage Negation, Special Boost, Damage Reduction, Not a Target, and Phasing. Normal applies to all others.

---

#### Stone Pool

Stone Pool gives the Artifact a fixed number of extra Attribute Stones after a Safe Haven Rest.

Choose one Attribute when the Artifact is created.

After a Safe Haven Rest, the Artifact contains Stones of that Attribute.

Once spent, these Stones are gone until the next Safe Haven Rest.

| Artifact Level | Stored Stones |
|---:|---:|
| 1-3 | 2 Stones |
| 4-6 | 4 Stones |
| 7-9 | 8 Stones |

Stone Pool must fit the Artifact's Slot and theme.

Unless the Artifact states otherwise, Stones from a Stone Pool can only be used for the Artifact's own Slot functions.

#### Stone Refresh

Stone Refresh restores spent Stones of one Attribute.

Choose one Attribute when the Artifact is created.

Stone Refresh can normally be used once per Safe Haven Rest.

| Artifact Level | Stones Refreshed |
|---:|---:|
| 1-3 | 1 Stone |
| 4-6 | 2 Stones |
| 7-9 | 4 Stones |

Stone Refresh is stronger than Stone Pool because it restores spent Stones.

The GM should decide carefully which Artifacts may use it.

#### Stone Battery

A Stone Battery is an empty storage vessel for Stones.

It starts empty.

It does not refill after a Safe Haven Rest.

It can only be charged through GM-approved sources.

| Artifact Level | Battery Capacity |
|---:|---:|
| 1-3 | 10 Stones |
| 4-6 | 20 Stones |
| 7-9 | 40 Stones |

A Stone Battery may be charged through:

- major story rewards
- rituals
- sacrifices
- defeated named enemies
- relic absorption
- other GM-approved sources

Minor enemies cannot normally charge a Stone Battery.

A defeated named enemy may provide Stones up to its Mastery Rank, if the GM allows it.

Stones stored in a Stone Battery keep the Attribute they had when stored.

Unless the Artifact states otherwise, Stones from a Stone Battery can only be used for the Artifact's own Slot functions.

## Artifact Summon Token Generator

Some Artifacts can strengthen the user's Summons by generating additional Summon Tokens.

These Tokens may only be spent on Summon upgrades.

They do not count as real Stones.

They do not increase the user's Stone Pool.

They cannot be used for Stone Abilities.

They cannot create a Summon Bond by themselves.

The character must already have at least one Summon created through a Bound Stone.

---

### Artifact Token Conversion

Artifact-generated Summon Stones are converted directly into Summon Tokens.

| **Artifact Effect** | **Summon Tokens Generated** |
|:--:|:--:|
| 1 Summon Stone | 4 Tokens |
| 2 Summon Stones | 8 Tokens |
| 4 Summon Stones | 16 Tokens |
| 8 Summon Stones | 32 Tokens |

These Tokens may be assigned to any Summon controlled by the character.

The assignment is chosen when the Artifact is prepared.

The assignment can be changed during a Bond Ritual.

#### Attribute Access by Slot

When an Artifact grants Attribute Stones, choose one of the Attributes allowed by its Slot.

The chosen Attribute is fixed when the Artifact is created.

| Slot Group | Allowed Attributes |
|---|---|
| Main Hand / Off Hand | Might or Agility |
| Body | Vitality or Might |
| Head | Wits or Intellect |
| Feet | Agility or Vitality |
| Amulet / Ring | Resolve or Intellect |

The GM may change the allowed Attribute if the Artifact has a strong concept, but this should be rare.

---

### No Cross-Slot Abuse

The Slot determines what an Artifact is allowed to do.

The description or appearance of an Artifact does not let it bypass Slot restrictions.

A ring shaped like a shield is still a Ring Artifact.

A cloak-like skin pattern does not create a Cape Slot.

A dragon scale cloak does not count as Body Armor unless it occupies Body.

A sword that heals must still be a Hand Artifact Active, not a general healing engine.

A Head Artifact that breathes fire uses an Active. Breath is not a separate category.

A Feet Artifact that teleports uses Movement. It does not also deal damage unless specifically written as a rare exception.

The Lantern of the Hollow Star is a bound Amulet Artifact shaped like a black lantern filled with pale inner fire.
