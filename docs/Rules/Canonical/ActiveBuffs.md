## Active Buff Rules

Active Buffs provide temporary combat enhancements. Choose **one Active Buff to maintain at a time**; its printed effects last for **Mastery Rank complete combat Rounds** unless its entry specifies another duration.

### Activation and Replacement

During a **Preparation Phase**, freely activate or replace an Active Buff. Activation uses **no Attack Action or Stones**; make a roll only if the Buff explicitly requires one. You may change your Buff even after confirming your Stones, keeping your confirmed Stone choices. End the previous Buff before the replacement begins. You may voluntarily end your Buff at any time.

The new Buff becomes active **before any creature takes its Turn** in the activation Round. Each **named Active Buff** may be activated **once per Combat**; when switching, choose a Buff whose activation is still unused. Powers with their own per-Combat uses retain their spent uses.

### Duration and Refresh

The activation Round is **Round 1**. An MR 2 Buff, for example, lasts the activation Round plus the following Round, and ends at the **end of its final Round**. Active Buff Empowerment extends this duration by its printed number of **complete Rounds** while keeping the Buff's original Power Level.

A Buff requiring maintenance remains active automatically. **Round-based** resources are received during activation Preparation and refreshed once during each later Preparation Phase while the Buff is maintained. For **Temporary HP**, the printed amount is restored up to its maximum; for **Damage Negation**, a fresh per-Round pool is available. These resources are established only once in a Round, including when switching Buffs. Other triggers occur at their printed time, such as **Start of Turn**.

At Combat End, maintained Buffs and their remaining temporary resources expire.

### Reading the Catalogue

Each heading identifies a distinct Buff Power. Its Power Level determines the strength shown in the table. A character may give the Buff a personal name while using the printed effect and normal Power Use Limit.

## Choosing an Active Buff

Each Active Buff strengthens **one feature** of your character, such as Armor, Evade, Damage, Cleanse, or a dedicated defense. Its complete effects and available Power Levels are shown in its own table.

### Defensive and Support Buffs

**Armor, Evade, Temporary HP, Spell Resistance, Self Cleanse, Invisibility, and Parry Recovery** provide their named defensive or support effect. Some enhanced defenses, including **Damage Reduction, Phasing, Absorption Stone Increase, and Damage Negation Pool**, require the matching slotted Passive.

### Offensive Buffs

**Damage** improves eligible damaging attacks. **Critical** and **Maximization** provide limited uses for eligible rolls. **Thorns** reflects damage after you take final HP damage. **Armor Reduction, Enemy Evade Reduction, and Spell Resistance Piercing** improve your own eligible attacks against the relevant defense. **Area Expansion** increases the radius of eligible instantaneous circular AoE attacks.

### Protective Auras

**Armor Aura, Evade Aura and Spell Resistance Aura** share their bonus with allies in the radius. Only the highest Active Buff bonus to the same value applies to a creature.

### Special Auras

The **fourteen named Special Auras** are Active Buffs that move with their caster. Each automatically applies its own printed Special to creatures in its radius under the shared Aura rules. Other Buffs provide their printed benefits without creating a Special application.

### Combining Effects

Resolve eligible bonuses from Passives, Stone Abilities, equipment and other sources according to their own rules. For **Critical** or **Maximization**, declare **one source per eligible roll**. For **Area Expansion** and **Intellect: Range & AoE Boost**, use the larger applicable radius bonus. The single maintained Active Buff slot and each source's printed limits always apply.

## Protective Auras (Active Buffs)

A **Protective Aura** is a maintained **Active Buff centered on you** that protects you and your allies. There are exactly three: **Armor Aura**, **Evade Aura** and **Spell Resistance Aura**.

### Activate and Maintain

Protective Auras follow the normal Active Buff rules: activate each named Aura **once per Combat** during Preparation at no Attack Action or Stone cost. It occupies your **one maintained Active Buff slot**, lasts **Mastery Rank complete Rounds** and moves with you.

### Creatures Affected

**You and every ally inside the radius** gain the printed bonus. Enemies are never affected. A creature gains the bonus as soon as it is inside the radius and loses it immediately when it leaves.

### No Stacking

A creature benefits from **only one Active Buff bonus to the same value** at a time. Compare its own Active Buff and every Protective Aura it is inside; **only the highest bonus** to Armor, Evade or Spell Resistance applies. Bonuses from Passives, Stone Abilities, Reactions, equipment and Artifacts are separate sources and add normally.

*Example:* Two allies each maintain an Armor Aura (+7 and +13). A third character inside both radii, with its own Active Buff: Armor (+8), gains only **+13 Armor** from Active Buffs.

## Special Auras (Active Buffs)

A **Special Aura** is a maintained **Active Buff centered on you**. It moves with you and automatically applies its **named Special(X)** to creatures in the printed radius. Each named Aura is learned as its own Power; its Level determines the printed Special value and radius.

### Activate and Maintain

Activate or replace an Aura **freely during Preparation**. It uses your **one maintained Active Buff slot** and lasts **Mastery Rank complete Rounds**, counting the activation Round first. The Aura follows your movement and remains active until its duration ends, you end it, or you replace it during Preparation.

### Creatures Affected

**You are immune to your own Aura.** Every other creature in range is affected, including **allies, enemies and Summons**. The Aura applies its printed Special automatically; resolve **Special Negation, stacking, recovery and Cleanse** as normal. Its application requires no attack or casting roll.

Apply the Special on **activation**, when a creature **enters the radius**, or at that creature's **Turn Start** if it has not yet received an application from that named Aura this combat Round. At Turn Start, resolve existing **Special Ticks, Natural Special Recovery and Decay** before that Round's Aura application. Each **caster + named Aura + target** combination applies at most **once per combat Round**. Remaining Specials follow their usual duration and recovery rules after the Aura ends.

### Special Details

**Root** begins at Root(2) and follows its own recovery rules. **Blight** is Timed. **Challenge** uses the Aura caster as its challenger. **Mark**, **Hex** and **Sundered** resolve according to their individual Special rules. Special Auras deliver **Specials** instead of direct Attack Damage; any later damage follows that Special's normal effect.
