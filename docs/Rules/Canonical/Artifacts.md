## Artifacts

This catalogue presents the Artifact rules and Level 1–10 profiles for **Destroyed Faith v0.9.9.6**. The Player Guide provides the shared combat, Stone, and Awakening rules; the Power Catalogues detail individual Powers.

**Hunter's Scourge** is described in the Player Guide. It grants Vitality: Damage Maximization Stone Power Support at Artifact Levels 2, 5 and 8 (Ranks 2, 3 and 4). Its Limited Scourge Strike may use an available Damage Maximization use. The character unlocks and activates the supported Ability normally.

Artifacts are bound objects, inherited manifestations, living relics, forged weapons, awakened armor, cursed tokens, supernatural body features, or similar relics that occupy real Equipment Slots.

Each Artifact has an **Artifact Capacity cost**, an **Equipment Slot**, a **Base Profile**, and a **fixed Level Progression**. Its listed functions are the abilities it grants.

**Artifact Damage and Delivery (Artifacts only):** A bound, equipped Artifact with a printed Damage value supports **any legal direct damaging Martial or Spell Delivery already available to its wielder**. For one eligible Damage Roll, choose **one** applicable Artifact's **complete printed damage contribution**, once. It never stacks with a second Weapon Damage, Spell Focus Damage, or Artifact Damage source. AoE and Autofire use that contribution once in their shared Damage Roll. **Mundane equipment is unchanged.** See *Unified Artifact Damage* below for the full boundaries.

**Echo Artifacts** follow these rules and remain bound to their character as part of their Echo.

---

#### Core Rules

By default, a character can be bound to up to **4 Artifacts** at the same time.

To bind an Artifact, both requirements must be met:

1. The character must have free Artifact Capacity.
2. The required Equipment Slot or Slots must be free.

Echo Artifacts count against Artifact Capacity.

Humans normally begin with no fixed Echo Artifacts and therefore keep their full Artifact Capacity open.

---

#### Equipment Slots

| Slot | Use |
|---|---|
| **Main Hand** | Main weapon, one-handed weapon, shield, hand focus, natural weapon |
| **Off Hand** | Shield, second weapon, offhand weapon, secondary hand item |
| **Body** | Armor, robes, natural armor, scales, plating, armored skin, body transformation |
| **Head** | Helmet, crown, mask, horns, eyes, senses, breath source, reaction focus |
| **Feet** | Boots, hooves, talons, movement organs, terrain movement |
| **Amulet** | Necklace, soul chain, oath mark, curse mark, spiritual binding |
| **Ring** | One magical ring, seal, minor binding, small ward, focused support |

Use the seven Equipment Slots listed above. Hand items occupy **Main Hand** or **Off Hand**; a focus uses the Slot of the Artifact that provides it.

### Binding and Awakening an Artifact

A newly acquired Artifact is initially **dormant**.

A character **binds an acquired Artifact directly** when they have sufficient Artifact Capacity and the required free Equipment Slot or Slots. Binding is free and awakens the Artifact at **Level 1**. Normal equipment handling during combat applies. Echo Artifacts selected at character creation begin already bound.

The Artifact immediately counts against the character's **Artifact Capacity** and occupies its required **Equipment Slot or Slots**.

---

### Artifact Progression

Artifacts have **10 Levels**. Advancement improves their Base Profile and unlocks or strengthens the functions listed in their Level Progression. At Level 10, an Artifact gains **Artifact Awakening** as described in the Player Guide. Its benefits refresh per **Safe Haven Rest**, with **one action-free Awakening Power per Round** across the character's Artifacts.

#### Artifact Level Cap by Mastery Rank

| **Mastery Rank** | **Maximum Artifact Level** |
|:--:|:--:|
| **MR 1** | **Level 1** |
| **MR 2** | **Level 2** |
| **MR 3** | **Level 4** |
| **MR 4** | **Level 6** |
| **MR 5** | **Level 8** |
| **MR 6+** | **Level 10** |

An Artifact may be advanced to the Level allowed by the character's current **Mastery Rank**. If MR decreases, the Artifact retains its earned Levels and resumes advancement once its Level is within the new cap.

#### Artifact Advancement Costs

| **Artifact Advancement** | **XP Cost** |
|:--|:--:|
| Bind and awaken a new Artifact at Level 1 | **Free** |
| Increase an Artifact to Level 2 or 3 | **8 XP per Level** |
| Increase an Artifact to Level 4, 5, or 6 | **16 XP per Level** |
| Increase an Artifact to Level 7, 8, or 9 | **32 XP per Level** |
| Increase an Artifact to Level 10 | **64 XP** |

During an Upgrade Step, an Artifact may gain **several Levels at once**. Pay the XP cost of every new Level reached, up to the character's MR-based Level cap.

#### Slot Blocking

An Artifact **occupies each of its listed Equipment Slots exclusively**, for both Artifact and mundane equipment.

Examples:

- A one-handed Artifact Weapon occupies Main Hand or Off Hand.
- A two-handed Artifact Weapon occupies Main Hand and Off Hand.
- Dragon Claws occupy Main Hand and Off Hand.
- Dragon Scales occupy Body.
- A magical ring occupies Ring. A character can bind only one magical Ring.

---

#### Two-Handed Artifacts

A two-handed Artifact counts as **one Artifact** for Artifact Capacity.

It occupies both **Main Hand** and **Off Hand**.

A two-handed Artifact has up to **three Base Values**, including Base Value C. A one-handed or versatile Artifact uses its normal Base Value limit even when held with both hands. Both hands remain occupied by the two-handed Artifact.

| Artifact | Artifact Capacity | Occupied Slots |
|---|---:|---|
| One-Handed Sword | 1 | Main Hand |
| Shield | 1 | Off Hand |
| Greatsword | 1 | Main Hand + Off Hand |
| Dragon Claws | 1 | Main Hand + Off Hand |
| Twin Shield Artifact | 1 | Main Hand + Off Hand |

A GM may approve a specialized **Twin Shield Artifact** for a suitable character concept.

---

#### Echo Artifacts

An Echo provides a defined selection of **Echo Artifacts**, including any required choices. Each uses Artifact Capacity and its printed Equipment Slots, and remains permanently bound as part of the Echo. For example, Dragon Scales occupy the character's Body Slot.

### Base Profiles and Artifact Functions

Every Artifact has a **Base Profile**.

The Base Profile defines what the Artifact is before supernatural progression is added.

The Base Profile describes the Artifact's inherent values, while its **Level Progression** grants Powers and other functions.

Base values belong in the Base table:

- Weapon Damage
- Armor Base
- Shield Value
- Head Armor
- Feet movement qualities
- similar slot values

List **Sense Options separately** beneath the Base Profile.

The Level Progression table is for unlocked Artifact Functions.

---

## Base Item Values

The **Base Item** describes up to three inherent **Base Values**, such as Damage, Armor, Evade, or Movement. The separate Level Progression lists Powers and Artifact Functions.

| Base Value | Unlock | Improvement | Final Improvement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Base Value A** | Level 1 | Level 4 | Level 7 |
| **Base Value B** | Level 4 | Level 7 | — |
| **Base Value C** | Level 7 | — | — |

Base Value A is the Artifact's primary identity.

Base Value B is a secondary feature.

Base Value C is a late base feature. A named Artifact may state a later unlock for a particular Base Value. **Level 10** grants Artifact Awakening.

### Base Value Limits by Slot

The Artifact's occupied Slot determines how many Base Values its Base Item may have.

| Slot | Maximum Base Values |
|---|---:|
| **Main Hand / Off Hand** | 2 |
| **Main Hand + Off Hand** | 3 |
| **Body** | 1 |
| **Head** | 2 |
| **Feet** | 2 |
| **Ring** | 3 |
| **Amulet** | 3 |

---
**The combined maximum number of Base Values printed on an equipped Ring and Amulet is 4.**

A one-handed Artifact uses up to **two Base Values**; a two-handed Artifact uses up to **three**. A Body Artifact uses one linked Base Value, which may include both **Armor and Evade** as defined by its Body Armor progression. Head and Feet Artifacts use up to two values; a Ring or Amulet uses up to three.

A character's equipped **Ring and Amulet together may have up to four printed Base Values**, including values that unlock at higher Artifact Levels.

The legal distributions are:

- Ring 3 + Amulet 1
- Ring 2 + Amulet 2
- Ring 1 + Amulet 3

Each Ring or Amulet is also individually capped at three Base Values. Echo Artifacts follow the same Slot limits. Additional functions belong in the **Level Progression**.

| Base Value Type | Examples |
|---|---|

The Slot's Base Value cap applies. A strong combined feature, such as **Thrown + Returning**, may use two Base Values.

### Artifact Level Structure

| Artifact Level | Stage |
|---:|---|
| 1-3 | Basic |
| 4-6 | Improved |
| 7-9 | Greater |
| 10 | Ultimate |

When an Artifact grants a normal Power through its Level Progression, use the following default Power Levels:

| Artifact Stage | Power Level |
|---|---:|
| Basic | Power Level 4 |
| Improved | Power Level 10 |
| Greater | Power Level 16 |

Artifact Level 10 grants the universal Artifact Awakening benefits defined in the current Core. **Artifact-granted Powers use their printed PL even if it exceeds the usual MR x 2 cap for learned Powers**, provided the Artifact Level is legal. For a granted Spell, calculate casting TN from the actual granted PL (PL x 4).

---

### Technical References and Power Identity

An Artifact Function has a **display name** and a **Technical Reference** identifying its canonical Power. **PL** is its effective Power Level.

- The referenced catalogue Power defines the function's complete mechanics, including timing, Range, action cost, triggers and resources.
- A granted Active Power can be used again whenever the character has another legal Attack Action, except where the Power explicitly limits its uses or expends a finite resource. There is **no generic once-per-Round per-instance limit**.
- **I, II, III** stages of one Artifact Function upgrade the same grant; they do not create duplicate charges, pools or Powers.
- Stone support, batteries, Sense Options, Active Buffs, Reactions and other Artifact Functions follow their own printed shared limits.

If a summary conflicts with its referenced catalogue Power, the catalogue controls unless the Artifact expressly provides an override.

---

#### Duplicate Power Sources

Different learned and Artifact-granted sources may grant the same canonical Power. They **do not** create extra Attack Actions, bypass shared charges or reset Combat-wide and Rest-wide restrictions. The same eligible Active may be used with successive legal Attack Actions without buying a duplicate copy. Resolve each granted source at its printed PL and obey ordinary Action Economy, Reaction timing and the single-maintained-Buff limit.

**Limited Powers retain one shared global Limited use per Safe Haven Rest.** Other expressly global resources remain shared.

---

#### Artifact Overlap and Retraining

If an Artifact grants a Power the character already learned, the existing paid Power is not automatically erased. A player may keep both sources, following ordinary legal-use rules, or retrain the learned Power and its paid upgrades during a permitted downtime opportunity for choices of equal or lower paid advancement value. Do not treat duplicate grants as additional Actions or replenished limited charges.

### Reaction Rules for Artifacts

If an Artifact grants a Reaction, it follows all normal Reaction rules.

A Reaction answers one immediate trigger.

A Reaction normally applies only to the triggering attack, hit, damage instance, defensive event, or declared Trigger.

If the referenced Reaction explicitly gives an effect a different duration, that specific duration controls. For example, remaining Temporary HP from **Reaction: Temporary HP** lasts until the end of the user's next Turn, and Initiative gained through **Reaction: Initiative** follows its own permanent Initiative rules.

A Reaction resolves its trigger as described by its Technical Reference, including any specifically stated duration.

---

#### Duplicate Reaction Sources

Each independently granted Reaction is a distinct instance with its printed Trigger and PL. A character normally uses **one Reaction per Round**; any specific action-free exception follows its own rule.

## Damage Baseline

If an Artifact grants a printed Damage value (Weapon Damage, Natural Weapon Damage, Bite, Claw, Tail, or another eligible direct attack value), use this default scaling unless the Artifact prints a different full progression. This value is **Artifact Damage** for the universal rule below, regardless of its original weapon or focus label.

| Artifact Level | Damage |
|---:|---:|
| Level 1 | 1d8 |
| Level 2 | 2d8 |
| Level 3 | 3d8 |
| Level 4 | 4d8 |
| Level 5 | 5d8 |
| Level 6 | 6d8 |
| Level 7 | 7d8 |
| Level 8 | 8d8 |
| Level 9 | 9d8 |
| Level 10 | 10d8 |

The table gives the Artifact's **complete Damage contribution**, unless its individual profile prints another total. Never add the base weapon dice twice. The Artifact's printed Damage is a single source for any legal direct Damage Attack; a separate Spell Focus category is **not** needed for that Artifact.

### Unified Artifact Damage (Artifacts Only)

An **equipped and usable Artifact** with a printed direct Damage value may add its **complete current Artifact Damage** to any **legal direct Damage Attack made by its wielder**: Melee, Ranged, or Spell. The character must already have the Power and its legal Delivery. Martial and Spell keep their own Attack/Casting Rolls, defenses, range and targeting rules; an Artifact never grants a new Power or Delivery.

- **One source per Damage Roll:** Select one eligible Artifact Damage source. Use its complete printed total, including any mundane weapon dice already incorporated into that Artifact total. Do **not** add a second Artifact's Damage, mundane Weapon Damage, or mundane Spell Focus Damage to the same roll.
- **Power Damage remains separate:** Add the Power's printed Damage and eligible bonuses normally. **Limited** multiplies **printed Power Damage only**, never Artifact Damage.
- **Shared rolls:** Add the selected Artifact Damage **once** to a shared instant Damage AoE or Autofire roll; reuse the result as the attack rules require. Separate qualifying Damage Rolls follow their own normal resolution.
- **No new damage category for effects:** Special-only Attacks (including Ruin application), Special ticks, Auras, Zones, forced movement, Restoration, Cleanse, Support and other non-Damage effects do **not** gain direct Attack Damage from an Artifact. An owner's Artifact does not empower a Summon's separately made attacks.
- **Mundane equipment is unchanged:** Ordinary weapons contribute Weapon Damage only when their existing Martial rules permit it; mundane Spell Focus Damage follows its existing Spell rules. This universal cross-Delivery permission applies **only to Artifacts**.

**In short:** The Power determines the effect and Delivery. A chosen Artifact contributes **one** eligible Damage value, regardless of whether the bearer describes their attack as a sword strike or a spell. It does not remove Power, Slot or Action requirements.

## Returning Weapon Baseline

If an Artifact Weapon is written as **Returning**, it returns to the wielder after a thrown attack or thrown Active granted by that Artifact is resolved.

Returning applies to attacks and Actives made **with that Artifact**, following their normal Action cost, targeting, Range and line-of-sight requirements.

## Weapon Special Baseline

An Artifact Weapon may gain a numeric **Weapon Special** through its Base or custom weapon profile. Use these Level-based progressions for its printed value. **Blight** uses Timed duration; **Weaken** and **Soulburn** reduce dice pools.

| Eligible Specials | Level 1 | Level 4 | Level 7 | Level 10 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Blight | 2 | 4 | 7 | 9 |
| Lacerate, Mark, Ruin, Slow | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Challenge, Corrode, Hex, Sundered | 1 | 3 | 4 | 6 |
| Disoriented, Expose, Soulburn, Weaken | 1 | 2 | 4 | 5 |

Use these separate baselines for Instant Specials:

| Special | Level 1 | Level 4 | Level 7 | Level 10 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Penetration | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Push / Pull | 2 m | 4 m | 6 m | 8 m |
| Precision | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Brutal Impact | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 |

**Finesse, Light, Reach, Thrown, and Returning** are fixed traits. For legal Spell Damage Attacks, use the Artifact's existing applicable damage value. An Artifact Weapon typically has **one full-value scaling Special**; additional Specials use reduced or staggered values. **Special-only Attacks** apply their Special and skip Attack Damage.

## Hand Artifacts

Hand Artifacts represent anything used through the hands.

This includes weapons, shields, natural weapons, claws, hand foci, and similar hand-based Artifacts.

Hand Artifacts use three Base Profiles:

1. **One-Handed Weapon**
2. **Two-Handed Weapon**
3. **Shield**

Parry tools, paired weapons, claws, gauntlets, weapon foci, and similar concepts must be written as one of these three profiles unless the GM creates a specific exception.

A shield used offensively is still a Shield.

---

#### Hand Artifact Base Profiles

| Profile | Slots | Artifact Capacity | Base Function |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **One-Handed Weapon** | Main Hand or Off Hand | 1 | Weapon Damage plus up to one additional eligible Base Value |
| **Two-Handed Weapon** | Main Hand + Off Hand | 1 | Weapon Damage plus up to two additional eligible Base Values |
| **Shield** | Main Hand or Off Hand | 1 | Shield Armor plus up to one additional eligible Base Value |

---

#### Weapon Actives

Main Hand and Off Hand Artifacts may grant Actives.

These Actives must be tied to the Artifact.

A Hand Artifact Active may include:

- Weapon Damage
- Restoration
- Cleanse
- Dispel
- Specials
- offensive spell-like effects
- shield strikes
- guard strikes
- other direct hand-based effects

Each Hand Artifact Active is linked to **one specific Artifact**. Apply that Artifact's printed effects to the Active.

## Shield Baseline

A Shield Artifact is a Hand Artifact.

It occupies Main Hand or Off Hand.

| Shield Base | Shield Value | Evade | Initiative | Training Penalty |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Parry Shield | +1 | +4 | - | -1d8 Physical Training checks |
| Medium Shield | +4 | +0 | - | -2d8 Physical Training checks |
| Tower Shield | +8 | -4 | -4 | -2d8 Physical Training checks |

Shield Value stacks with Armor Value as normal Armor resolution.

A Shield Artifact may grant defensive Actives, Reactions, Stone Functions, guard effects, shield strikes, or protect effects.

An explicitly **offensive Shield Artifact** may also have a printed Weapon Damage progression.

## Body Armor Baseline

Magical Body Armor is built from a normal Armor chassis.

Choose **Light Armor**, **Medium Armor**, or **Heavy Armor** when the Artifact is created. A Robe is handled separately under **Robe Artifacts**.

The chosen Armor type determines the Artifact's starting Armor, its normal Evade modifier, and any Initiative or Training penalties.

| Body Base | Starting Armor | Base Evade Modifier | Initiative | Training Penalty |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Robe / No Armor | 0 | +0 | - | - |
| Light Armor | 4 | +0 | - | - |
| Medium Armor | 8 | -2 | -4 | -1d8 Physical Training checks |
| Heavy Armor | 12 | -4 | -8 | -2d8 Physical Training checks |

Apply the chosen Armor type's printed benefits and drawbacks to every Body Armor Artifact, whatever its appearance. An individual Artifact may specify an exception.

---

#### Building Body Armor Artifacts

Each Body Armor Artifact uses the **complete Level progression** of its chosen Armor type below.

- **Light Armor** uses linked Armor and Evade progression. Armor begins at **4** and reaches **8**. Evade begins at **+2** and increases by **+1 every Artifact Level**, reaching **+11** at Level 10.
- **Medium Armor** uses linked Armor and Evade progression. Armor begins at **8** and reaches **12**. Its Artifact Evade Bonus begins at **+1** and increases by **+1 every two Artifact Levels**, reaching **+5**. The normal Medium Armor **-2 Evade** modifier still applies.
- **Heavy Armor** is primarily Armor. Armor begins at **12** and reaches **16**. It keeps its normal **-4 Evade** modifier, but from Artifact Level 6 onward it gains a fixed **+2 Artifact Evade Bonus**, reducing the final Armor-based modifier to **-2 Evade**.

For all three Armor types, Armor increases by **+1 every two Artifact Levels** after Level 2.

The printed **Armor total and Evade modifier** together form **one Body Armor Base Value** at each Artifact Level.

For Medium and Heavy Armor, calculate the final Evade modifier by combining the Armor type's normal Base Evade Modifier with the Artifact Evade Bonus shown in its progression table.

Apply the Armor type's Initiative and Physical Training penalties at every Level, with any explicitly printed Artifact-specific adjustments. Use the listed Armor and Evade progression as written.

### Artifact Sense Options

Artifacts may grant **Sense Options**.

A Sense Option is a small perception feature that sits beside the Artifact's real mechanics.

A **Sense Option** grants an additional sensory channel. Write it separately beneath the Artifact's Base Item. It is available at its stated range and follows the **Special Sense Slot** rules. Each Artifact normally provides **one Sense Option**; any additional options are explicitly listed in that Artifact.

---

### The Character's Special Sense Slot

Every creature always uses **Normal Combat Awareness** through its available ordinary senses. It also has **one Special Sense Slot**, initially empty.

Artifacts, Echoes, Powers, and other features may provide Special Combat Senses. **Choose one available Special Sense** before the first Perception roll of the scene, or before Initiative in combat. It remains selected for that scene or combat, following the usual switching rules.

The chosen Special Sense works **in addition to** Normal Combat Awareness. A creature can detect a target through any active, applicable Sense; the Sense must still have legal range and a viable sensory channel. Darkvision upgrades Normal Combat Awareness, leaving the Special Sense Slot available. See the Core **Combat Senses** rules for Clear, Obscured, Blocked and Perception.

#### Attribute Access by Slot

When an Artifact grants Attribute Stones, choose one of the Attributes allowed by its Slot.

The chosen Attribute is fixed when the Artifact is created.

| Slot Group | Allowed Attributes |
|---|---|
| Main Hand / Off Hand | Might or Agility |
| Body | Vitality or Might |
| Head | Wits or Intellect |
| Feet | Agility or Vitality |
| Amulet / Ring | Resolve or Intellect |

The GM may change the allowed Attribute if the Artifact has a strong concept, but this should be rare.

---

### Slot Examples

An Artifact uses the **functions of its Equipment Slot**, regardless of its visual form. A ring shaped like a shield remains a **Ring Artifact**; a protective scale cloak uses the **Body Slot** for Body Armor. A sword restoring Scarred Health Bars uses a **Hand Artifact Active**, a fire-breathing helmet uses a **Head Artifact Active**, and teleporting boots use a **Movement Power**.
